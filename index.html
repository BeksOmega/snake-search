The S.N.A.K.E. Safety Rules

Remember these five simple steps whenever you encounter wildlife on the trail:

compare_arrows Harmless Swimmer vs Venomous Viper

🌿 Plain-bellied Water Snake (Harmless)
  • Swims submerged with just head poking out
  • Round, friendly pupils
  • Vertical black bars on lip scales
  • Plain orange/yellow unpatterned belly
⚠️ Cottonmouth Viper (Venomous)
  • Swims high and buoyant on surface like a pool noodle
  • Vertical cat-like slit pupils
  • Dark bandit mask across eyes
  • Flashes bright white mouth lining when startled
Texas Coral Snake Rhyme

"Red touch yellow, kill a fellow; Red touch black, friend of Jack!"

Harmless Texas scarlet snakes have red touching black bands, while venomous Texas coral snakes have red touching yellow bands. Always admire both from a safe distance!
